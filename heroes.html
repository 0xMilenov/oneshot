        How to Play

        Goal

        Capture the enemy's capital city, or wipe out all their heroes and towns. Lose yours, and the crown is theirs.

        Adventure Map

        Click your hero to select them, click a destination to plan a path, click again to travel. Movement points refill each day. Pick up resources, chests, and artifacts; step onto mines to claim their daily income; step onto monsters to fight them. Right-click / hover anything for details. Pan with arrow keys/WASD or by dragging; click the minimap to jump.

        Towns

        Each town can construct one building per day. Build dwellings to unlock creatures, halls for gold, the mage guild for spells. Creature stocks replenish every 7 days (each new week). Move troops between garrison and a visiting hero. Don't leave towns undefended!

        Combat

        Battles are on a hex grid, ordered by creature speed. Click a highlighted hex to move; click an enemy in reach to attack (melee strikes take one retaliation). Shooters attack anyone at range unless an enemy is adjacent. Your hero may cast one spell per round — press S. W waits (act later this round), D defends (+30% protection). Auto resolves the fight instantly.

        Heroes

        Victories grant experience. Leveling raises primary stats and offers a choice of secondary skills. Artifacts and mage guild visits make heroes stronger. Necromancers raise a share of the fallen as Skeletons.

        Keys

        E end turn • H next hero • T town • V hero screen • C center • Esc close/deselect
